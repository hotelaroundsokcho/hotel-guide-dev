지하주차장에서 짐을 먼저 내리신 후 이동해 주시기 바랍니다.

임의로 주차하실 경우 주차요금이 발생할 수 있으니 꼭 주의 부탁드립니다.
The underground parking lot is shared by Hotel Around Sokcho and Marivista Hotel.
On weekends and during peak season, the number of parking spots allocated to our hotel may be exceeded.
If exceeded, even an empty-looking spot is not available for our guests.

We will text you in advance whether underground parking is available.
If not available, we will also send a link to a free public parking area ↗ about a 5–6 minute walk away.
Repositioning your vehicle takes about 90 minutes.
Please unload your luggage first in the underground parking lot before moving your car.

Please note that parking without guidance may result in parking fees — thank you for your understanding.
